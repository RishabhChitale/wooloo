Headpats and hearts for the companion, both driven by new optional parts of the local manifest (without them nothing changes, and no artwork or sound is included). With `headpat`, a pointing hand shows over her head and clicking it is one pat: it plays the pat sound (every third pat also plays the second sound; the new "Headpat sounds" checkbox in Webcam Settings turns the sounds off), counts in a session that ends after 20 seconds without a pat, and steps her mood up by how many pats she got, with a line in the speech bubble for six seconds on each new step; hovering shows the reaction's hover animation. The reached mood holds for five minutes (four seconds for "done" when she was working), a newer reply of Ame on JINE replaces it, and `state/mood.json` carries it. When a session of three or more pats ends and Ame is free, she gets one hidden `[pat]` turn (at most once per ten minutes, never in the quiet hours), which the JINE list hides like nudges. With `hearts`, a small pink bar with a heart sits at the top left of the room, over cutscenes too: it starts at 40, falls with the hours (also while the app is closed), and rises with each pat, each new loving or happy reply of Ame, and each new loving message of yours, and drops with her sad or angry replies; every message is counted once, also across relaunches. When a change takes the bar up to 100 she plays her nap clip once (or its fallback) with "zzz... ♡" in the bubble, and then goes back to her mood; the level stays at 100, it fires again only after the bar has dropped below 100 and climbs back, and launching at 100 fires nothing. Her idle behaviours now depend on the hearts instead of the clock: the hours, idle-minutes and either conditions are gone, each ambient entry takes `minHearts` and `maxHearts`, the wait before a clip is 90 to 180 seconds at no hearts and 25 to 60 at full hearts (rests of 180 to 300 down to 30 to 90 seconds), and the sleep clip is no longer an idle behaviour.
