Changing the companion's skin can now play a transformation cutscene: when the manifest in the local companion folder lists a `transitions` entry for the old and new skin (an exact pair, or any skin to the new one), its full-scene frames play once in her room, filling it like the background, before the new skin shows. Frames are decoded a few at a time and released afterwards, a new skin change replaces a cutscene in progress, and skins without a cutscene switch instantly as before. A "Play transformation when changing skin" checkbox in Webcam Settings (on by default) turns it off. No artwork is included.
