First release of the RishabhChitale/wooloo fork, based on upstream wooloo 0.3.16.

- The Spaces list now also sits at the top of the right sidebar (Files and Changes), so Spaces stay reachable with the left sidebar hidden.
- New Space menu items: Next Space (Option-`) and Previous Space (Option-Shift-`), cycling through Spaces in sidebar order.
- Updates now come from this fork's own signed feed. A daily workflow merges each new upstream release and publishes a matching fork release.
