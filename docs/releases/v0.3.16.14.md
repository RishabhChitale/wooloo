The companion now does things on her own while she is calm. When the local manifest lists `ambient` entries (a clip key such as `tweet`, `sleep` or `comics`, the moods it suits, a weight, and optional hours and idle-time conditions), then after being idle, done or waiting for 25 to 60 seconds she picks one the current skin has a clip for, plays it for 20 to 45 seconds, rests for 30 to 90 seconds and may pick another, never the same one twice in a row. Any change of mood, skin or activity, a cutscene, or a game or stream activity stops it at once. While a clip plays its key is what `state/mood.json` reports and what Ame's unprompted texts say she is doing. An "Idle behaviours" checkbox in Webcam Settings (on by default) turns it off. No artwork is included.

The left sidebar (the Spaces list) now starts hidden and stays however you last left it, so relaunching or opening a new window never brings it back by itself; View > Show Sidebar (Ctrl+Cmd+S) still works, and opening the session picker still shows it.

Animation switches are now instant: no crossfade and no eased zoom, a new clip's first frame is read the moment it starts so the scene never shows without her, and the zoom is centered on one steady area per skin (where she appears over her base and emotion animations) so changing animations never moves or rescales her.
