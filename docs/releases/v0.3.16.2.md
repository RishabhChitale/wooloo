Adds a companion panel at the top of the right sidebar: a small animated character that follows the status of the Space's commander agent (working, waiting, done or idle) and speaks in a bubble, showing the commander's terminal title while it works. Its artwork is not included in wooloo: the frames and manifest are read from a local folder (`~/.local/share/wooloo-companion/ame`, or the folder in `WOOLOO_COMPANION_DIR`), and the panel stays hidden when that folder is missing.
